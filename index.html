3. Advanced TCP Flags and Attack Vectors

Certain TCP flags can be exploited for reconnaissance:

  • SYN Flood: Sends SYN requests without completing the handshake
  • FIN Scan: Sends FIN packets to identify closed ports
  • Xmas Scan: Sends FIN, PSH, and URG flags simultaneously
